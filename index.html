About Involve

Who We Are!

Involve Cultural and Charitable Society is a forum of enthusiastic and like-minded people who have joined together with a vision to create a platform for supporting various social, cultural and educational activities.

  • Our Story

    It started with a group of nostalgic companions who wanted to have a feeling of being socially involved for good causes and the welfare of the society. Starting in 2016 January 26th, the initial group of fifteen members, our team has been subsequently joined by a limited number of friends with a common interest in social welfare activities.

  • Our Mission

    Our mission is to offer a helping hand to individuals, families and communities by strengthening the social, communal, educational and cultural base in sustainable ways. Our mission is not only to help human kind but to contribute towards the wellbeing of our planet and its ecosystem.

  • Our Vision

    Our vision is to pave a strong foundation for overall social development initiatives and also to promise social justice to all. We make sure the development and enhancement of such noble social causes through capacity building, developing mechanism and service delivery.

    Green Project

    Involve Green is an eco endeavour by Involve to promote nature friendly living. It is a green initiative that encourages the society to embrace nature by promoting the origination and maintenance of vegetations such as Trees, Green Spaces, Tiny Forests, Big Forests and Agricultural Farms.
