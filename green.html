Involve Nature Cell

Green Project

Involve Green is an eco endeavour by Involve to promote nature friendly living. It is a green initiative that encourages the society to embrace nature by promoting the origination and maintenance of vegetations such as Trees, Green Spaces, Tiny Forests, Big Forests and Agricultural Farms. This Project will appreciate the people who contribute to our nature and inspire others to do the same by creating and maintaining different types of Green Life in their surroundings.

Green Life we promote

  • Trees
  • Green Spaces
  • Tiny Forests
  • Big Forests
  • Agricultural Farms

Read about our larger dream on the Involve Forest page.
